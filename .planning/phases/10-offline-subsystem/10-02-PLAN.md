# Plan 10-02: Offline Database & Watch Progress Sync Queue

## Objective

Build structured offline metadata indexing and progress synchronization queue (`OFFL-02`):
1. Store metadata for completed offline media (`itemId`, `title`, `overview`, `localPath`, `fileSize`, `durationTicks`, `posterPath`).
2. Queue watch progress events generated while playing offline (`itemId`, `positionTicks`, `isPlayed`, `timestamp`).
3. Support query, update, and removal of pending progress entries.
