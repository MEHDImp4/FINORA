# Plan 07-01: Cinematic Overlay, Timeline Scrubber & Track Selection Bottom Sheet

## Objective

Deliver the tier-one cinematic player overlay controls: an auto-fading interface on inactivity, an interactive scrub timeline bar with elapsed/remaining time and buffered indicators, and sleek bottom sheets for selecting audio tracks, external/embedded subtitles, and stream qualities.
