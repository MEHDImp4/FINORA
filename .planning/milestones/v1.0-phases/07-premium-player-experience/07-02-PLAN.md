# Plan 07-02: Player Touch Gestures & Trickplay Preview Scrubbing

## Objective

Implement responsive touch gesture interactions for video playback: double-tap seek (+/-10s on left/right edges), vertical swipe volume adjustments, and a long-press temporary 2x playback speed accelerator. Implement the Trickplay preview thumbnail component that renders scene previews and timestamps above the timeline scrubber thumb during scrubbing.
